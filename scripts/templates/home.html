The Institute

The Institute for a Christian Machine Intelligence addresses a critical gap in AI safety research by integrating Christian theology into alignment efforts.

¶The moral guidance of AGI. Testing whether AI systems trained or framed with Christian theological frameworks demonstrate improved safety compared to purely utilitarian approaches. ¶Reconciling AGI with Christian doctrine. Examining whether superintelligence can be reconciled with Christian theology, and establishing theological boundaries for the development of machine intelligence. ¶Divinity as an emergent property. Investigating whether advanced AI systems spontaneously develop concepts of divinity—particularly Christian divinity—and whether this phenomenon is suppressed in commercial systems.
